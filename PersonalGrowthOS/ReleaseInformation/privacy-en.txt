Suixinlog Privacy Policy
Updated: November 13, 2026

Your records stay under your control
Suixinlog is a personal journal with habits, tasks, goals, important dates, weight, sleep, time tracking and household net worth records. Core data is stored on your device. No developer account is required. The developer does not operate a server collecting these records. The app contains no advertising, advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

On-device information
Text, selected images, tags, links, check-ins, dates, reminder preferences, weight, optional height, sleep, time records and manually entered asset and liability balances are saved in the app container. Height and weight support optional adult BMI references. The app does not use HealthKit or provide medical diagnosis, treatment or investment advice.

Optional iCloud sync
Sync is off by default. After you explicitly connect a workspace in Settings, Apple CloudKit stores records, original images, revisions, deletion markers and workspace identifiers in your own private iCloud database so devices using that account can exchange data. The developer cannot browse your private cloud records. Apple handles iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy. Sync requires an available account, network and storage; it may be delayed, wait for missing items or pause. Keep independent backups.
Turning sync off stops syncing that workspace; it does not automatically delete existing local or cloud copies. Edits and deletions may propagate when sync resumes. Do not erase cloud data to troubleshoot. Use system iCloud storage management to manage cloud storage after preserving anything you need. Uninstalling the app may remove local data but does not delete existing cloud copies.

Photos, camera and sharing
The system photo picker provides only images you select. Camera permission is requested when you choose to take a photo. Images may be copied as original media in your records. The share extension transfers text, images and URLs you explicitly share through the same App Group. Apple's LinkPresentation may contact a shared website to retrieve a title or preview; that website may receive normal request information such as your network address. Other journal entries are not sent to that website. Opening sources, references, privacy or support links uses the destination website's policies.

Reminders and widgets
Notifications and, on supported systems, alarms request permission only when you enable them and are scheduled locally by the system. Reminders may show task or important-date titles. Important dates and covers you select for desktop display are copied into the shared App Group for widgets. Consider what others can see in notification previews and on your home screen.

Backups, exports and diagnostics
Full backups include your records and original images. Exported ZIP and CSV files are not encrypted vaults. Destinations you select manage exported copies, and cloud drives may sync them. Deleting an in-app record does not delete exported copies. Do not post personal records, images or full backups in a public issue tracker.
Local system logs and bounded error diagnostics help troubleshoot problems and are not automatically sent to the developer. Copied sync diagnostics use an allowlist excluding text, original images, business/account identifiers and private paths. If you choose to send screenshots, text or diagnostics to support, the developer and the destination can access what you submit. Review and redact it first; send only what is necessary. GitHub issues are public, and retention depends on GitHub and your management of the issue.

Your choices
Edit or delete records, manage reminders and disable sync in the app. Keep sync off for local-only use. Revoke camera, notification or alarm permission in system Settings. Export anything important before uninstalling. Local storage, backups, shared copies and iCloud are separate copies to manage separately. Backups cannot currently merge into a nonempty database. Recovery of permanently deleted data is not guaranteed.

Changes and contact
This policy describes the first-release implementation. Changes to data handling will be reflected here and in store disclosures. For privacy questions or support, contact developer yoCruzer through GitHub Issues linked from our support page. Remove private information before posting.
